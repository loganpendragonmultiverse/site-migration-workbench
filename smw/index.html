Freeze the reviewed plan

            

              Freezing preserves this revision's scope, provenance, exceptions,
              environment settings and content hash. It records an operator
              decision, not a signature. Later edits make the working copy
              different; old revisions and their runs remain intact.
            

            

            
            
Deployment capability boundary

            

              Nginx exact-path and WordPress Redirection exports support
              conservative ASCII, query-free source paths, 301/308 redirects and
              supported retirement statuses. Unsupported query, encoded,
              Unicode, cross-host source or unsafe paths block the entire
              adapter export with a manual instruction. Plan JSON/CSV always
              retain every row. Generated rules are for operator review and
              deployment.
